# Prior-art search protocol: Silo Swarm

Search date: 2026-10-14 UTC

## Target under review

A local visual demonstration that:

1. partitions incident evidence across four visually represented silos;
2. performs 16 parallel typed probabilistic AI judgments;
3. performs a connected verification wave over pooled evidence;
4. uses deterministic host policy to turn judgments into an executable response and illustrative superadditive utility; and
5. emits data-minimized, HMAC-chained audit events with explicit assurance boundaries.

The review separates the novelty of this **combination and demonstration** from novelty of any component, theorem, user-interface theme, TypeSafe/Jev model, or general multi-agent architecture.

## Claims tested

- **C1 — Scientific novelty:** Does the demo establish a new scientific method or result?
- **C2 — Technical novelty:** Is the implemented architecture absent from known systems and publications?
- **C3 — Demonstration distinctiveness:** Is the exact integration and presentation uncommon enough to be differentiated?
- **C4 — Compliance novelty:** Are its audit controls new, rather than an application of established secure-logging controls?

## Inclusion and grading

A result is retained when its title, abstract, documentation, or claims overlap at least one target component. Results are graded:

- **Direct prior art:** substantially the same end-to-end combination.
- **Close adjacent art:** overlaps multiple central elements or the same workflow without the presentation/audit combination.
- **Component prior art:** establishes that one element is already known.
- **Background:** useful context without a material novelty effect.

Exact-match absence is not evidence of patentability or scientific novelty. A defensible patent opinion would require professional claim construction, family and citation review, and jurisdiction-specific legal analysis.

## Exclusions

- Generic “AI swarm” marketing without technical details
- Generic dashboards unrelated to agent execution or evidence aggregation
- Ordinary SOC 2 descriptions with no AI/audit relevance
- Agricultural silos and unrelated Bloom-filter results
- Duplicate versions of the same work

## Reproducibility and limitations

The search script stores queries, timestamps, identifiers, URLs, and snippets. Search-engine ranking and indexes change. Semantic Scholar, direct arXiv, and Google Patents APIs were rate-limited or unavailable during initial probes, so OpenAlex plus patent-restricted web search are used as fallbacks. Non-public systems, unpublished patent applications, and terminology not covered by the query families may be missed.
