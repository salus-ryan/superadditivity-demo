## Interpretation limits

Keyword coverage is a retrieval signal, not legal anticipation. Jev probabilities are semantic triage signals, not legal findings. OCR recovered all five documents but omitted some printed claim-number labels, so the 91 machine-extracted claim segments are not guaranteed to equal the legal claim count even where the underlying text is present. A single earlier reference generally must disclose every properly construed limitation for novelty; obviousness may rely on combinations and motivation to combine. Abstracts, snippets, and automated OCR cannot replace independent-claim review against an authoritative patent record. Proxy-family grouping by title and priority date is not a substitute for INPADOC family analysis. Legal status shown by public databases may be incomplete or delayed. Unpublished applications create an unavoidable blind spot.
